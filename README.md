# Adam Roubeni

Founder of **[Void](https://voidbetfree.com)** · building trading systems, automation and consumer apps · New York

- 📱 **Void:** quit-gambling app on the App Store, with Android in Google Play testing. Blocks sportsbook apps with Apple Screen Time, plus urge support, check-ins and group challenges.
- 📈 **Trading systems:** real-time engines for Kalshi sports and crypto markets, with WebSocket order books, calibrated win-probability models, fee-aware edge detection and risk controls.
- 🎟️ **Automation:** high-concurrency checkout and inventory-monitoring bots for live-event tickets (over $10M in sales), plus large-scale scrapers.
- 🤖 **AI:** LLM agents and AI automation across Claude, GPT and Gemini.
